# Leo's Little Wings

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Leo. Leo had small, brown wings.

"I want to fly to the top of the tall mountain," Leo said.

His friend, Felix the falcon, flew fast. *Whoosh!* "I am fast! I will get there first!" Felix laughed.

Felix flew high. But soon, Felix got tired. His wings hurt. "This is too hard!" Felix cried, and he went home.

Leo flew slowly. *Flap, flap, flap.*

"Pace yourself, Leo," whispered the wind.

When the rain fell, Leo rested under a leaf. When the rain stopped, Leo flew again.

*Flap, flap, flap.*

He was slow, but he did not give up.

Step by step, flap by flap, Leo reached the top of the mountain. He saw the beautiful red sunset.

"I did it!" Leo chirped. "Slow and steady wings can climb the highest peaks!"
