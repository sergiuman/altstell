# The Heart Keepers

One quiet morning, Kiri sat by the river with her painting supplies. She wanted to paint the flowers, but her chest felt heavy. It felt like she had a cold, wet stone sitting inside her heart. Her eyes felt stingy, and she didn't want to paint at all. 

"What is wrong, Kiri?" asked Pip, sitting on a branch above her. "Are you sick?"

"No," Kiri whispered, looking down. "I just feel... a grey feeling in my heart. But I don't know what it is."

Sola, the ancient willow, lowered her soft, green branches to wrap around Kiri like a warm blanket. 

"Kiri, my child," Sola whispered, "inside every heart lives a tiny family of forest spirits. They are called the **Heart Keepers**. They are your feelings. Sometimes they are happy, and sometimes they are sad, angry, or scared. They want to be noticed."

"Spirits inside my heart?" Kiri asked, wiping her eyes.

"Yes," Sola smiled. "Look closely at your chest. Who is visiting you today?"

Sola showed Kiri how to close her eyes, take a deep, slow breath, and look inside. In the quiet of her mind, Kiri saw a tiny, blue spirit shaped like a little rain cloud. It was sitting on a leaf, dropping tiny water droplets and sobbing quietly.

"That is **Cloud (Sadness)**," Sola explained. "When you feel sad, Cloud is visiting. Do not throw him away, and do not lock him in a dark box. He just wants you to say hello."

Kiri opened her eyes and smiled gently at the feeling in her chest. She sang:

*“Welcome, little spirit, sit on my shoulder today,*
*Tell me your story, I’m listening to what you say!”*

As soon as Kiri welcomed the blue spirit and allowed herself to shed a little tear, the heavy stone in her chest felt lighter. Cloud stopped crying, curled up on her shoulder, and fell asleep.

"Who are the other spirits, Sola?" Pip asked, jumping with curiosity.

"Ah," Sola said. "There is **Spark (Anger)**. He is a bright red spirit who jumps and sparks like a campfire. When things are not fair, Spark comes out. If you try to fight Spark, he gets hotter. But if you take three deep, slow breaths—like blowing out a candle—Spark cools down into a warm, cozy light."

"And what about when I feel wiggly and want to run away?" Pip asked.

"That is **Shiver (Fear)**," Sola replied. "He is a tiny grey spirit who hides behind a big fern leaf. He is very quiet. When Shiver visits, your heart beats fast. He doesn't want you to run. He just wants you to hold a friend’s hand and know that you are safe."

"My favorite must be the yellow one!" Kiri giggled.

"Yes," Sola laughed. "That is **Butterfly (Joy)**. She is bright yellow, she tickles your belly, and she makes you want to jump, paint, and share your toys. When Butterfly is here, the whole world looks bright!"

Sola looked at the children. "Remember, no spirit is bad. Even Spark and Cloud are important. But you must never hide them in a dark cage. If you hide them, they get noisy and hurt your chest. Instead, call them by their names. Invite them to sit on your shoulder, and ask them what they need."

Kiri nodded. She looked at her canvas. She felt Cloud sleeping peacefully on one shoulder, and Butterfly starting to tickle her belly. 

She picked up her paintbrush. She did not paint only bright yellow flowers. She painted a beautiful blue river, a warm red sun, and a cozy grey tree. All the colors were there, and it was the most beautiful painting she had ever made.

*“Welcome, little spirit, sit on my shoulder today,*
*Tell me your story, I’m listening to what you say!”*

From that day on, whenever Kiri, Pip, or Ursu felt a big feeling, they stopped, took a deep breath, and looked inside. They called the spirits by their names, listened to their stories, and kept their hearts warm and open.
