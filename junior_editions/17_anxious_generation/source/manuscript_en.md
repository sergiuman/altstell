# The Play Forest

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Dan.

Dan had a little magic mirror. The mirror was shiny and had bright lights.

Dan sat on his chair and looked at the mirror all day.

He did not run. He did not jump. He did not climb.

His heart felt tired and sad.

An old toy-maker named Eldon said: "Dan, let us go to the Play Forest. Leave the mirror here."

They went to the green forest. Dan met his friends.

They climbed a wooden log. They jumped over a muddy puddle. *Splish, splash!*

They laughed and ran in the warm sun.

Dan's heart felt happy and strong.

He looked at his muddy hands. "The real forest is more fun than the shiny mirror!" Dan laughed.

And they played all day!
