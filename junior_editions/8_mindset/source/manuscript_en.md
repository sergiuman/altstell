# The Little Seed that Loved to Try

Once upon a time, in a big, green forest, lived a little seed named Pippin. Pippin lived in warm, brown dirt.

Pippin looked up at the tall oak trees. "I want to grow tall too," Pippin whispered.

But growing was hard. One day, Pippin tried to push a tiny green leaf out of the dirt.

Pippin pushed and pushed. But the dirt was too hard. "Ouch," Pippin said. "I failed. I am not a good seed."

A friendly worm named Wally crawled by. "Do not worry, little seed," Wally said. "Try again tomorrow. Trying makes you strong."

The next day, it rained. The dirt became soft. Pippin pushed again. *Pop!* A tiny green leaf came out.

Then the wind blew hard. The wind bent Pippin's stem. "Oh no," Pippin cried. "The wind is hurting me!"

But Wally the worm smiled. "No, Pippin. The wind is teaching your roots to hold on tight."

Pippin held on tight. Day by day, Pippin tried and grew, leaf by leaf.

Now Pippin was a beautiful little sapling, waving in the wind. "I love to try!" Pippin sang.
