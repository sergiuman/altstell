# Victor's Strong Shield

Once upon a time, there was a little hedgehog named Victor.

Victor was scared. "I am too small. I cannot do hard things," Victor said.

A big, strong badger named Grizz smiled. "Yes, you can, Victor. Your mind is stronger than you think."

Grizz asked Victor to climb a steep path.

"My feet hurt!" Victor said.

"Say to yourself: I am strong like a rock!" Grizz said.

Victor took a deep breath. "I am strong like a rock," he whispered.

The rain fell, but Victor kept walking.

*Step, step, step.*

He reached the top of the hill. He was very happy!

"I did it!" Victor said. "My little paws are strong, and my mind is like iron!"
