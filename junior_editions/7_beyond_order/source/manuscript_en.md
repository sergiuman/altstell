# The Cozy Nest and the Wild Woods

In the beautiful valley of Eldoria, two friends had very different ideas about where to live. 

Mr. Bramble, the old grey badger, lived in a tidy root lodge under the great oak tree. He loved order. Everything in his home had a perfect place. The tea mugs were lined up by size. The floor was swept clean of every speck of dust. The blankets were folded neat and square. 

"Nothing must change," Mr. Bramble whispered happily, sitting in his clean armchair. "A tidy house is a safe house."

But outside, beyond the whispering valley, lay the Wild Woods. 

Pip, the little red squirrel, loved the Wild Woods. He did not care about tidy rooms. He loved adventure! He spent all day leaping from tall pine branches, looking under thick green moss for secret mushrooms, and exploring dark caves. 

"Rules are boring!" Pip chattered, throwing acorn shells on the grass. "The wild forest is where the magic is!"

But sometimes, their ideas brought trouble. 

In his tidy root lodge, Mr. Bramble began to feel very lonely and bored. No one ever visited, because they might bring mud on their paws. The room was silent. Day after day, everything looked exactly the same. Mr. Bramble felt like a prisoner in his own clean house.

Meanwhile, out in the Wild Woods, Pip climbed a tree that was too tall. The wind blew hard, a branch snapped, and Pip fell into a thorny blackberry bush. He hurt his paw. It began to rain, and Pip had no roof, no warm dry blanket, and no fireplace. He sat under a cold leaf, shivering and scared.

Kiri found Pip and carried him back to the valley. They sat near Sola's warm branches, where Mr. Bramble was taking a quiet walk.

"Look at us," grumbled Mr. Bramble. "My house is too quiet, and Pip's forest is too dangerous."

Sola rustled her leaves like a soft lullaby. "My friends, you both have half of the secret. Mr. Bramble has the Cozy Nest, which is safe and clean. Pip has the Wild Woods, which are exciting and new. You need both to be happy."

"We need both?" Pip asked, holding his bandaged paw.

"Yes," Sola said. "Without a cozy nest, you get cold, tired, and lost in the wild. Without the wild forest, you get bored and dusty in the quiet. You must have a clean nest to rest in, so that you have the courage to go out and explore the wild."

Kiri picked up Mr. Bramble's broom and Pip's backpack. She sang:

*“A cozy nest to rest our head,*
*A wild forest to explore instead!”*

Mr. Bramble and Pip looked at each other. They understood. 

The next day, they worked together. Pip went to Mr. Bramble’s lodge. He learned to wipe his paws on the grass and help sweep the floor. They made the root lodge warm and clean, folding the blankets together.

But in the afternoon, Mr. Bramble put on his sturdy walking boots. He took Pip by the hand, and they walked out *beyond* the whispering valley, into the Wild Woods. 

Pip showed Mr. Bramble a hidden clearing where wild white flowers grew. Mr. Bramble showed Pip how to safely climb the rock hill. They had a wonderful adventure, finding new berries and listening to the birds.

When the sun went down and the forest grew dark and cold, they did not stay in the wild. They walked back to the warm root lodge. They drank sweet chamomile tea from clean mugs, wrapped themselves in soft, folded blankets, and sat by the crackling fire.

Pip looked at the clean floor, then out at the dark, wild forest. He felt safe, and he felt excited for tomorrow.

"Goodnight, Mr. Bramble," Pip whispered, closing his eyes.

"Goodnight, Pip," the old badger smiled.

They had found the perfect balance: a clean, safe nest to sleep in, and a wild, magical forest just outside the door.

*“A cozy nest to rest our head,*
*A wild forest to explore instead!”*
