# Quick-Hop and Slow-Hoot

Inside a little boy's mind lived two little animals.

One was Quick-Hop the bunny. Quick-Hop was very fast. *Boing, boing, boing!*

The other was Slow-Hoot the owl. Slow-Hoot was very slow. *Hoot, hoot.*

One day, they saw a big red strawberry in the grass.

"Yum! I want to eat it!" Quick-Hop said, running fast.

"Wait, bunny," Slow-Hoot whispered. "Let us look closer."

Quick-Hop did not wait. He bit the strawberry. *Yuck!* It was made of hard clay!

"Oh," bunny said. "It just looked like a strawberry."

Later, they heard a loud noise: *Grrr!*

"A monster!" bunny cried, hiding behind the owl.

Slow-Hoot looked closely. "No, bunny. It is just a friendly frog snoring under a leaf."

"Oh," bunny smiled.

"We must work together," owl said. "Bunny is fast, and Owl is wise."

Now, they walk together, happy and safe!
