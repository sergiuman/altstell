# Kindness in the Forest

Once upon a time, in a snowy wood, walked a little boy named Ben.

Ben was cold.

A little mole came out of the snow. "Here is a warm hug," mole said.

They walked together. They saw a quiet fox. The fox was sad.

Ben gave the fox a yellow flower. The fox smiled and walked with them.

Then they met a big white horse.

"I am tired," Ben said. "Can you help us?"

"Yes," the horse said. "Get on my back."

They rode through the snow, safe and warm.

"What is the best thing in the world?" the mole asked.

"Kindness," the horse said. "Being kind to your friends, and being kind to yourself."

They were happy, and they were home.
