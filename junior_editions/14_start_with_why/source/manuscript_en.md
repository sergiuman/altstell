# Maya's Golden Compass

Once upon a time, a little girl named Maya had a golden compass.

Maya wanted to build a toy boat.

"What color?" asked Leo. "Red!"

"How to build?" asked Sonia. "With nails!"

But Maya stopped. "Why do I want to build a boat?" she asked.

She closed her eyes. "Because I want to see the blue lake and find new friends."

That was her *Why*.

Because she wanted to find friends, she made a big deck for them to sit on.

Because she wanted to see the lake, she made tall sails to catch the wind.

Her boat went very far, straight to the friendly ducks on the lake.

"Start with your dream," Maya said, "and your boat will always find the way!"
