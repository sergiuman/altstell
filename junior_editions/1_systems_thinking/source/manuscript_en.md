# Radu and the Magic Glasses

Once upon a time, in a small wooden house near a big, whispering forest, lived a little boy named Radu. Radu had curly brown hair, bright eyes, and a very big curiosity. He loved to count ladybugs, measure the length of twigs with a wooden ruler, and ask questions that made grown-ups scratch their heads. 

Radu believed that if you looked closely enough, you could see everything in the world exactly as it was. 

Radu lived with his grandfather, Tudor. Grandfather Tudor was very wise. He did not have a smartphone, but he knew the name of every bird, the secret paths of the forest, and how to bake the warmest apple pies.

One sunny morning, Grandfather Tudor sat on the porch. On the table, he had a beautiful, shiny box made of cherry wood. Inside the box, resting on soft green velvet, were five round, colored glass lenses. They looked like giant, magical eyes.

"What are these, Grandfather?" asked Radu, hopping on one foot. "Are they magic glasses?"

"Yes, Radu," Grandfather Tudor smiled. "They are magic lenses. They show you different secrets of the world. Pick one up and look at our garden."

Radu picked up the red lens and held it to his eye. He gasped! 

The blue sky turned a deep, dark purple, like nighttime. The green leaves of the apple trees looked grey and dusty. But the round, red apples in the trees shone like bright, glowing stars! They were so easy to see!

Radu took off the lens, looked at the red glass, and sang:

*“Look closely once, look closely twice,*
*The magic glasses change your eyes!”*

"Grandfather, the garden is red!" Radu laughed. "And the apples are like little suns!"

"Now try the green one," said Grandfather Tudor.

Radu swapped the red glass for the green glass. Oh, my! The garden changed again! Now, the red apples disappeared completely, blending into the dark branches. But the green leaves glowed with a beautiful, bright emerald light. Radu could see every tiny line and vein on the leaves, like a secret map.

*“Look closely once, look closely twice,*
*The magic glasses change your eyes!”*

"The garden is green now, Grandfather! The apples are gone, but the leaves are so bright! Which lens is telling the truth? Is the garden red, or is it green?"

Grandfather Tudor sat down next to Radu and patted his shoulder. "Both are telling the truth, my boy. The red lens helps you find the fruit to eat. The green lens helps you see if the leaves are healthy. Each lens shows you a different part of the same garden. None of them shows you everything, but both are very useful."

"But Grandfather, what if I put all the lenses together?" Radu asked. He stacked the red, green, blue, and yellow lenses on top of each other and held the thick pile to his eye. 

He saw nothing. It was completely black!

"Aha!" Grandfather Tudor laughed softly. "If you try to look through every lens at the same time, you only get darkness and confusion. The secret is not to wear all of them at once. The secret is knowing when to swap them."

The next day, Radu went to play in the forest meadow. He met his friend, Andrei. Andrei had a big orange soccer ball and a paper notebook. 

"I have the perfect plan to win our soccer game against the kids from the next hill!" Andrei said proudly. He showed Radu his notebook. It was full of blue and red arrows. "We must stand exactly here, pass the ball in a perfect triangle, and shoot. If we follow this plan, we cannot lose!"

Radu nodded. The plan looked neat and clean. But when the game started, a new boy named Vlad joined the other team. Vlad was as fast as a rabbit and ran everywhere! He did not stand where Andrei’s arrows pointed. 

Andrei's team got confused. They kept looking at the paper plan and bumping into each other. Soon, they were losing two to zero.

At halftime, Andrei was very sad. "My plan is perfect! Why isn't it working?"

Radu thought of his grandfather's magic box. He realized Andrei was looking through the 'Plan Lens'—he only saw the paper drawings, not the real, moving kids on the grass!

"Andrei," Radu said, "your plan is like a map of a forest that stands still. But soccer is a forest that runs! Let's swap our lens. Instead of looking at the paper, let's follow one simple rule: *Watch the kid closest to you, and pass to whoever is free.*"

They went back onto the field. They stopped looking at the paper and started looking at each other. The game became easy and fun! They passed quickly, ran fast, and scored three goals. They won the game!

After the match, Andrei smiled. "Your simple rule was much better than my big plan, Radu."

"The plan was good for planning, but the simple rule was good for playing," Radu explained. 

*“Look closely once, look closely twice,*
*The magic glasses change your eyes!”*

When Radu returned home, he sat under the big nuc tree with Grandfather Tudor. He held the green lens up to the sky.

"Grandfather," Radu said, "I think our minds have magic glasses too. Sometimes we look at things in one way, and we think it is the only way. But if we change our lens, we can see things in a new way and solve our problems."

"Yes, Radu," Grandfather Tudor said proudly. "Those are called **mental models**—the glasses of our minds. Remember, never get stuck wearing just one color. Always be ready to look through a new lens to see the beautiful, colorful world."

Radu smiled, put the green lens safely back in its velvet box, and looked out at the forest. It was green, and grey, and full of secrets, waiting for the next magic lens.
