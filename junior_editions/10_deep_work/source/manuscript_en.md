# The Quiet Forest

Once upon a time, in a bright forest, lived a little squirrel named Pip.

Pip wanted to build a wooden house for the winter.

But the forest was very busy. "Look at the butterflies!" Pip said, dropping his hammer.

"Listen to the frogs!" Pip said, running to the pond.

At the end of the day, Pip had no house. Only one stick on the ground. He was cold.

An old, wise bear named Barnaby walked by. "Pip," Barnaby said. "If you want a house, you must find a quiet place and do deep work. No butterflies. No frogs. Just building."

The next day, Pip went to a quiet corner of the forest. He closed his ears to the noise.

He focused only on the wood and his hammer. *Tap, tap, tap.*

He did not run away. He worked and worked in the quiet.

Before sunset, Pip had a cozy, warm wooden house.

"Liniștea," Pip sang, "is my helper!"
