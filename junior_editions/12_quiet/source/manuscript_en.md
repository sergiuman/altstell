# Oliver's Quiet Power

Once upon a time, in a busy pond, lived a little beaver named Oliver.

Oliver was very quiet. He loved to look at the trees and listen to the water.

His brother, Buster, was very loud. "Look at me! Shouted Buster. *Slap, slap!* went Buster's tail.

One day, the big river rose. The dam had a tiny leak.

Buster ran in circles. "Help! Shout! Run!" Buster made a lot of noise.

But Oliver walked slowly. He looked closely at the leak.

He found a small stick and a piece of soft clay.

Without making any noise, Oliver plugged the leak. The water stopped.

Buster stopped shouting. He looked at Oliver. "You are quiet, Oliver. But your quiet hands saved us."

Oliver smiled and gave Buster a warm hug. Quiet power is beautiful too!
