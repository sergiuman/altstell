# The Sleep Elves

Once upon a time, inside a cozy bedroom, lived a little boy named Toby.

Toby did not want to sleep. "No sleep!" Toby said. "I want to play!"

But his eyes were heavy. *Yawn!*

He closed his eyes, and *pop!* he met the Sleep Elves.

The Elves had tiny golden brooms. "We must sweep the dust from your brain, Toby!" they said.

Other Elves had tiny paintbrushes. "We must paint rose on your cheeks!"

Other Elves had little hammers. "We must make your muscles strong so you can run tomorrow!"

The Elves worked all night while Toby slept warmly in his bed.

In the morning, Toby woke up. He felt happy, strong, and full of energy!

"Thank you, Sleep Elves," Toby whispered. Sleep is magic!
